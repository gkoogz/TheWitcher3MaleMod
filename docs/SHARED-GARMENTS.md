## Walking-sheet candidate

The current development replaces the earlier radial pouch with one continuous
fabric sheet. Its broad upper seam follows an 80-degree total arc along the
lower front waistband. Surface walks use authored shaft, glans and both scrotal
lobe groups, continue around the complete anatomy, then beneath the lobes and
return to two short underside strap seams. Interior cloth uses persistent
constraints and unilateral contact, rather than fixed anatomy pins.

Two continuous smooth hems run along the walked side edges. They use the same
cloth particles as the sheet, with elastic tension to draw the sides mostly
closed while leaving small intentional openings. The upper/bottom attachments
are sewn seams; they are not part of a single closed perimeter hem.

The top and bottom waistband edges follow independent transverse cuts of the
actual body surface. Strap origins sit at the lateral hip midpoint, then follow
measured paths around the glute fold and medial thigh. MMGRB003 adds the four
authored tissue index groups without changing any native anatomy draw indices.
MMGFC006 diagnostics record expanded exact garment lineage and the measured
relative anatomy mass used to derive fabric mass. Versions 001 through 004
retain their original four donors; 005 retains expanded lineage and supplies
the historical unspecified mass. Captured inputs do not include prior
persistent cloth history and cannot alone prove a dynamic replay.

The MMGRE001 companion records the source mechanical ownership of each
retained native anatomy vertex, pinned to the same Base revision and render
packet as MMGRB003. This interpolates measured source fields; it is not the
full nonlinear surface Jacobian. Actual unilateral cloth contacts produce
equal-and-opposite impulse and moment. Wire 6 carries cumulative generalized
impulses, which the source consumes once across asynchronous publications,
retries and pauses. Contact conservation, source consumption and clean-pin

## Installed clothing prototype

Naked is the default and disables cloth and support. Clothing cycles between
Naked and Jockstrap in F6 with the usual arrow keys. A separate preference
persists the selection; private verification never writes user preferences.

The white jockstrap has a solid waistband with separate red and blue stripe
meshes, a ribbed pouch, small hem and two measured rear strap paths. Its pouch
uses the current anatomy surface rather than deleting or substituting it.
Waist and pouch expansion preserve measured body boundaries and donor lineage.
Every vertex is connected to actual body/anatomy donors. The shared fit checks
anatomy coverage and body-capsule contact budgets; bounded source physics
feedback gives support only while clothed.

shape-preserving capsule projection, spacing and physical sewing repair. Numerical fitting runs
asynchronously. Presentation transports the fitted residual through current
weighted donor positions and the complete current native bone pose. The
historical 2062b2d presentation fixture measured about 0.48 ms; this is not a
current full cloth-worker or gameplay FPS claim. The corrected Base gate covers
96 actual source states and 41 measured Geralt control/articulated binding states.
Serialized source contact work averaged 57.675 ms; native scheduling and rendered
appearance remain separate adapter gates.
See GARMENT-PRESENTATION.md and GARMENT-RENDERER-GATES.md.
